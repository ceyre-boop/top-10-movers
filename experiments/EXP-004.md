# EXP-004 — Liquidity / microstructure features (V2)

- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Counts toward family-wise correction? (y/n)**: y
- **Variant:** 2 of a pre-registered ladder
- **Spend:** $0 — no new data, computed entirely from bars already on disk

## Hypothesis
Explosive movers have identifiable structural preconditions. Thin
liquidity is one: a thin order book amplifies a given inflow of capital.
Adding microstructure features should therefore improve T1 over EXP-003.

## Features added (5)
All derived from daily OHLCV already purchased — no vendor, no PIT risk,
`as_of` inherited from the 16:00 ET bar stamp.

- `log_amihud` — Amihud illiquidity, mean of `|ret| / dollar_volume` over 20d
- `cs_spread_20d` — Corwin-Schultz spread estimator from daily high/low
  (notable: estimates the bid-ask spread with no quote data at all)
- `zero_vol_20d` — count of zero-volume sessions in 20d
- `adv_pct_rank` — cross-sectional percentile of dollar ADV
- `range_20d` — mean intraday `(high-low)/close` over 20d

Coverage 97.4%–100%. Median Corwin-Schultz spread 0.96%, median intraday
range 4.1% — both plausible for a $1M-ADV small-cap universe.

## Result: NULL

hits/day, expanding-window walk-forward, yearly retrain:

| | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2 (5d realized vol) | 0.534 | 0.514 | 0.713 | 0.587 |
| V1 — T1 only (EXP-003) | 0.625 | 0.570 | 0.783 | 0.660 |
| V2 — T1 + liquidity | 0.725 | 0.574 | 0.758 | 0.686 |
| **V2 − V1** | **+0.100** | **+0.004** | **−0.025** | +0.026 |

**Read this as a null result, not a +0.026 improvement.** The mean is
positive only because 2020 is. The year-over-year trajectory decays to
*negative*: +0.100 → +0.004 → −0.025. That is the same shrinking-margin
signature EXP-003 showed against B2, and the pre-declared pass/fail
signal for this plan was explicitly the trend, not the average.

**Feature importance confirms it.** Not one of the five liquidity
features appears in the top 8 by gain:

```
xs_vol_rank      16,484,819     <- 7x the next feature
xs_ret_rank       2,257,659
top90             1,397,952
logpx             1,318,921
dist52              917,197
vol20               848,330
vol5                803,890
relvol_prev         773,869
```

The model was handed all five and effectively ignored them.

## Interpretation

The liquidity half of the "loaded spring" hypothesis does not add signal
in this universe. A plausible reason: the universe filter already
selects on liquidity (prior close >= $1, 20d ADV >= $1M), so the
surviving cross-section has had most of its liquidity variation removed
before the model ever sees it. The feature is real; the variance is not
there to exploit.

This does not refute the structural-precondition thesis generally — it
tests only the liquidity component. Short interest and crowding are
tested separately in EXP-005.

## Note on 2020

2020 is where the entire mean delta comes from, and 2020 is the COVID
regime. A feature family that helps only in the single most anomalous
year of the sample is a regime artifact, not an edge.
