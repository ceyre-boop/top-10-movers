## The P2 number worth remembering

**54.8% of eligible common stock and ADRs are delisted.** A survivor-only
dataset would be missing more than half the population — and the missing
half skews toward exactly the low-priced, high-volatility names that
dominate top-gainer lists. This is why P2 is a kill criterion rather
than a caveat.

The daily cross-section pull is survivorship-free by construction, and
the Polygon delisted list now provides an independent check on that
rather than relying on inference alone.

## Correction to an earlier decision recorded here

An earlier revision of this file recorded a Nasdaq-only dataset and
argued for discarding a partial NYSE chunk, on the grounds that a
time-varying universe definition is worse than a narrower constant one.

That reasoning was sound but the premise was wrong: it assumed a hard
$2 budget cap that turned out to refer to Anthropic API spend, not
Databento. The ingest was resumed and completed. All three venues now
span the full period, so neither the narrowing nor the time-varying
universe applies. No LABEL_SPEC deviation needs recording in
`docs/RESULT_TOP10.md`.

## Cost findings retained

- `definition` schema over this range prices at **$180.31** — more than
  the entire $125 credit. Free per-day `symbology.resolve` is the only
  viable symbol-resolution path, and per-day is mandatory because
  Databento reassigns equity instrument_ids daily.
- Every instrument_id is resolved, with no dollar-volume pre-filter. A
  filter would halve wall time but could drop a normally-liquid name
  having a quiet session, corrupting the universe denominator and every
  rank computed from it.
- Cross-venue volume still understates consolidated volume (ARCX, IEXG,
  MEMX and others are not included), so the $1M ADV filter remains
  stricter than LABEL_SPEC intends. Closing prices are unaffected — the
  official closing auction runs on the listing venue, which is the close
  the build uses.
